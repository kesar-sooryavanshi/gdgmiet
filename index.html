Who We Are

About HackHeist

HackHeist is a high-octane, developer-focused gathering organized by GDG on Campus MIET designed to ignite your passion for technology, innovation, and community problem-solving.

Whether you are writing your first line of code or deploying complex full-stack architectures, HackHeist is your launchpad. Over an action-packed journey, students come together to learn modern technologies through interactive workshops, listen to seasoned mentors during tech sessions, forge lifelong friendships, and build production-ready projects in our flagship hackathon.

No boring lectures, no gatekeeping — just pure creativity, teamwork, caffeinated coding, and the thrill of turning ideas into reality.
