About The Event

What is HackHeist?

A developer-focused community gathering where curiosity meets practical engineering.

HackHeist is an annual developer-focused gathering organized by the student community at GDG on Campus MIET. It brings together curious students, programmers, and tech enthusiasts to learn, build, collaborate, and connect.

Whether you are taking your first steps into code or regularly building personal projects, HackHeist provides an open and welcoming environment. Over the course of the event, participants take part in hands-on technical workshops, gain actionable career and engineering insights during tech talks, build lasting connections with fellow students, and bring their ideas to life in a fun, collaborative hackathon.

There is no barrier to entry — all students with an appetite for learning and problem-solving are welcome to join.

Hackathon

Build and showcase your ideas. Work in teams to build functional prototypes solving real-world challenges, with constructive feedback from faculty and mentors.

Workshop

Learn practical development skills. Follow along in guided sessions covering modern web development, Git fundamentals, API integration, and cloud basics.

  • Step-by-step hands-on coding
  • Starter templates provided

Tech Session

Learn from experienced developers. Listen to talks from senior engineers and community leads about practical software engineering and navigating tech careers.

Networking

Connect with students and the developer community. Meet peers across departments, find team members for upcoming projects, and join the local GDG chapter.
